How Much Does SEO Cost in Pakistan?

SEO services in Pakistan typically cost PKR 25,000 to PKR 100,000 per month for ongoing retainers, and PKR 15,000 to PKR 60,000 for a one-time audit and technical fix. The exact number depends on three things: how competitive your keywords are, how much work your site needs, and what the agency actually includes. Local SEO for one city is cheaper than national SEO for competitive keywords like "web design services."

As a rough guide:

  • PKR 25,000–50,000/month: local or small-business SEO — on-page work, content, basic links, and reporting.
  • PKR 50,000–100,000/month: competitive niches, e-commerce, and national SEO with regular content and stronger link building.
  • PKR 100,000+/month: large sites, aggressive link building, and enterprise-level campaigns.
  • One-time audit: PKR 15,000–60,000 to find and fix technical issues without an ongoing contract.

What You Actually Pay For

A real SEO retainer should include more than "monthly reports." Expect:

  • Technical SEO. Fixing site speed, crawl errors, mobile issues, and structure.
  • Keyword research. Finding the searches your customers actually make.
  • On-page optimization. Titles, descriptions, headings, and content built around those keywords.
  • Content creation. Articles and pages that earn traffic — this is the biggest part of modern SEO.
  • Link building and citations. Earning mentions and links from real, relevant sites.
  • Local SEO. Google Business Profile, reviews, and local citations if you serve a city.
  • Reporting. Honest updates on rankings, traffic, and leads — not vanity numbers.

Cheap SEO vs Quality SEO

Factor Cheap SEO Quality SEO
Price Very low, often "unlimited" promises Fair, transparent pricing
Methods Spam links, keyword stuffing, shortcuts Clean, Google-safe techniques
Results Short-term bumps, then penalties Steady, lasting rankings
Risk Site can get de-indexed Low — protects your site
Communication Generic reports, no real work Clear reporting and honest updates

The uncomfortable truth: if SEO is extremely cheap, the work being done is probably spammy — and it can get your website penalized by Google, losing all rankings and traffic. Cheap SEO is often more expensive in the long run because you'll pay to fix the damage.

When to Pay for SEO — and When to DIY

You can handle some basics yourself: writing quality content, optimizing your own pages, and setting up a Google Business Profile. For low-competition niches with a tight budget, DIY can work. But technical SEO, link building, and competitive strategy need real expertise — and the time you'd spend learning SEO could be better spent running your business.

Pay for SEO when you're in a competitive niche, when rankings directly drive your revenue, or when you've tried DIY and stalled. Most serious businesses find that a good SEO retainer pays for itself many times over.

How to Get ROI from SEO

  • Set realistic expectations. SEO compounds over 3–6 months; judge it on leads, not week-one rankings.
  • Ask what's included. Get a written scope — content, links, technical work — not vague promises.
  • Beware of guarantees. No honest agency can guarantee #1 rankings for competitive keywords.
  • Track leads, not just rankings. Traffic only matters when it becomes enquiries and sales.
  • Stay patient. The businesses that see the biggest SEO wins are the ones that stay consistent.
"The best SEO investment isn't the cheapest — it's the one that builds rankings safely and keeps them. Cheap shortcuts are the most expensive SEO there is."

Key Takeaways

  • SEO in Pakistan costs PKR 25k–100k/month; one-time audits run PKR 15k–60k.
  • Price depends on competition, niche, and what the service includes.
  • Cheap SEO uses risky tactics that can penalize and de-index your site.
  • A real retainer covers technical SEO, content, links, local SEO, and honest reporting.
  • SEO is a 3–6 month compounding investment — judge it on leads, not week-one rankings.
