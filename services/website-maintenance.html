A website isn't a one-time project — it needs care to stay fast, secure, and ranking. AH Digital Solutions provides website maintenance services in Pakistan — backups, security, updates, and content changes — for businesses in Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, and across the country. What Website Maintenance Includes

Think of us as the care team for your website. Everything technical, handled so you can focus on your business.

  • Security monitoring & updates. Your site protected against hacks, malware, and vulnerabilities.
  • Regular backups. Automatic backups so your site can be restored in minutes if anything goes wrong.
  • Content updates. Text, images, prices, and pages updated whenever you need.
  • Speed optimization. Your site kept fast so visitors don't leave and Google keeps ranking you.
  • Bug fixes. Broken links, forms, and errors fixed quickly.
  • SEO health checks. Regular checks to make sure your site stays visible on Google.

Why Maintenance Matters

A neglected website gets slower, breaks more, and loses rankings. Here's what happens without regular care:

  • Security risks. Outdated sites are easy targets for hackers — a hack can take your business offline.
  • Losing customers. Slow or broken pages push visitors to your competitors.
  • Lost rankings. Google favors fast, secure, well-maintained websites.
  • Outdated content. Old prices or information lose you trust and sales.
