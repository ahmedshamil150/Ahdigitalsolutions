Why Website Speed Matters

Speed is money. Studies consistently show that even a one-second delay in load time reduces conversions and satisfaction. A fast website:

  • Ranks higher on Google. Speed is a confirmed ranking factor.
  • Converts more visitors. Faster pages mean fewer people leaving before they buy.
  • Keeps visitors engaged. Slow pages frustrate users and send them to competitors.
  • Performs on mobile. Most Pakistani customers browse on phones — often on slower networks.

What Our Optimization Services Include

  • Performance audit. A full analysis of your site's speed scores, issues, and bottlenecks.
  • Image & asset optimization. Compressed, properly sized images that load fast without losing quality.
  • Code optimization. Clean, efficient HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Caching & CDN. Faster delivery to visitors, wherever they are.
  • Core Web Vitals. Fixing LCP, INP, and CLS to pass Google's experience metrics.
  • Ongoing monitoring. Keeping your site fast as you add content and features.
